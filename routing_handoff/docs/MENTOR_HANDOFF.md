## Integration agreement needed
Mentor owns forecasting. Routing accepts supported flame-contact and incident heat-flux arrays, not raw weather or images. Supply a sample with schema/model version, graph revision/CRS/grid, units (seconds, kW/m², kJ/m²), issue/availability/validity/time origin, full support masks, stable scenario IDs and provenance. Agree destination dwell, route-only versus including-dwell dose, peak/dose policies, incurred-dose source and changed-member history bounds. Arrival/probability-only predictions need a separate defensible physical adapter; a surrogate remains assumed physics. Missing support is not zero hazard.

The exact schema and example are in the package INTERFACE.md and fixtures/example.json. First test ingestion, then initial route, progress with nonzero incurred dose, forecast update, replan, stale result rejection and checked return. Serialize session mutation/commit; worker inputs must be immutable snapshots. Forecast integration remains pending until an actual sample is tested.

## Results and default
Keep the established road planner as the research reference. The packaged compatible-input library defaults to its full-vector baseline; these are different interfaces. The sealed Nangok research study resolved 482/482 queries (380 routes, 102 refusals), using generated hazards. Zero-exposure screening reduced median paired cold time about 7.8%, with no resolution gain; all final road exposure budgets were nonbinding. The optimization is not demonstrated merged into this shipped package.

Latest H1 weighted-bound V1 gave a wrong optimum; repaired V2 has no confirmatory performance result. H2 reuse increased aggregate planning time 10.62%; neither is promoted. A proposed fixed-route forecast-tolerance postprocessor is a new experiment, not delivered functionality. It needs full dose/interior-extremum handling and update guards; feasibility reuse does not preserve optimality.

## Evidence boundaries
Road geometry is real historical OSM; hazards/scenarios/destination conditions are generated and travel conditions assumed. No actual mentor integration, independent thermal-road truth, physical safety validation, universal A*/Dijkstra superiority or new-algorithm claim. MoE findings are fixtures, not Korean benefit evidence. One vehicle route is not fleet evacuation performance.

Meeting outcomes: first sample/date; agreed units/support/times; adapter owner; one region/update sequence; independent evaluation evidence; paper claim scope. Main research question: efficient computation and updating of hazard-constrained escape routes under conflicting scenario budgets, motivated by authorities assisting vulnerable residents.
