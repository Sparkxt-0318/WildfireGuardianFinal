**Completed: 18 hypotheses, six rapid mechanism tests, three candidate authors and a separately authored adversarial evaluator. No new generic algorithm, new fire law, real-fire prediction improvement or delivered-system replacement was earned.** There are worthwhile research questions; there is no justified breakthrough headline. ## Recommendation and strongest idea

**PH1, topology-preserving passage summaries, is the strongest fresh representation research lead in this candidate set.** Equal fuel fractions can conceal a connected corridor versus an impassable barrier. Under a prescribed stationary directed graph with nonnegative additive costs, boundary-to-boundary shortest-path summaries preserve queried arrival exactly. Independent evaluation passed 192 comparisons; the equal-fraction twins yielded arrival 3 versus unreachable. This targets a specific information-loss mechanism rather than another undifferentiated model replacement.

It is potentially stronger than scalar fuel-fraction aggregation because that statistic cannot distinguish those twins. **It has not been demonstrated stronger than the actual current WildfireGuardian direction:** the scalar comparator deliberately discards geometry, the mentor model was not tested, fine fuel geometry and calibrated spread delays are unavailable, and the boundary-clique algorithm is established Customizable Route Planning. A homogeneous author example reduced vertices 64→30 while increasing arcs 224→228; independent overlays increased arcs in 54/192 checks. Changed weights invalidated a cached answer (2 versus updated truth 10).

Next falsifier: a matched-input stationary repeated-query experiment, charging fine-graph construction, summary preprocessing, tables, every query and updates. Stop if realistic inputs do not support the representation, connectivity does not affect the chosen endpoint, or total cost regresses. **Recommendation: retain as a gated larger experiment; no forecasting integration.** Do not claim a Rothermel advance: these are prescribed-graph arrival results, not observed rate-of-spread evidence.

**AL2 is the most practical next executable study:** an offline irreducible budget-conflict explanation with replayable removal witnesses. It passed 54 independently constructed finite routing fixtures and exhaustive subset checks. A separate control proves it can return a two-member conflict while a singleton conflict also exists: inclusion-minimal is not minimum cardinality. Unknown or capped oracles remain UNRESOLVED. The next test must use exposure-active cases and the delivered route contract, preserve all-member hard constraints, and independently replay witnesses. Current Nangok cases with nonbinding exposure budgets do not establish usefulness. This is established conflict diagnosis applied to a new project question, not new search mathematics. No member is dropped from an operational decision.

**DS1 is the strongest research-method finding:** one final perimeter admits 81 perfectly fitting integer-grid worlds with route-relevant arrival from 35–55 minutes. Two timed observations resolve an example only under constant-speed assumptions; piecewise-speed twins still share both perimeters and ignition while arriving at 45 versus 55 minutes. The next action is an evidence specification for eligible independently timed fronts and uncertainty, not another architecture name. Missing thermal truth remains missing.
